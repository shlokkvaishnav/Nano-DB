THE SHAPE THE TRUNCATION HID (5,000-object runs, head + tail)
==========================================================================
  characterize.py keeps the first six samples and the last three. Put
  them side by side and the trajectory is not a ramp at all.

  seed 35888  (repair_s 38.286, 17 samples total)
    head: 0.0s:140  2.4s:2500  4.7s:2500  7.1s:2500  9.4s:2500  11.7s:2500
    ...gap the harness did not store...
    tail: 32.0s:2500  35.0s:3741  38.3s:5000
    -> 2500 of 5000 objects arrive by t=2.4s (50%), then the count sits at 2500 until t=32.0s,
       then climbs to 5000 by t=38.3s.
  seed 35949  (repair_s 40.787, 22 samples total)
    head: 0.0s:13  1.7s:1432  3.8s:2000  5.7s:2000  7.4s:2000  9.2s:2000
    ...gap the harness did not store...
    tail: 35.0s:2026  38.1s:3741  40.8s:5000
    -> 1432 of 5000 objects arrive by t=1.7s (29%), then the count sits at 2000 until t=35.0s,
       then climbs to 5000 by t=40.8s.

  So the trajectory is BURST -> ~30 s PLATEAU -> FINAL CLIMB, and the
  quantity reported as 'a ~6 s ramp' is only the final climb. It is
  not a transfer duration and it is not the whole shape.

  NOTE this also breaks the segmentation rule SPEC.md registered,
  which assumes the first probed count IS the plateau count. Here the
  first probe (140, 13) catches the burst instead, so the same rule
  would report a zero plateau -- which is exactly what it does on
  dissociation seed 20260903 below. A registered rule that mis-fires
  on a known case is reported, not quietly patched.

THE REGISTERED CONTROL IS NOT AVAILABLE
==========================================================================
  SPEC.md registered the dissociation study's no-chaos control arm as
  the noise floor: a control run's count should be flat across the
  window. 5 control runs are committed and 0 carry a
  completeness_series -- the harness samples the series only in the
  chaos branch, so the registered control DOES NOT EXIST in the data.
  Reported rather than silently substituted.

  The available substitute is internal and weaker: each chaos run's
  own PLATEAU is a flat reference measured by the same probe on the
  same corpus. If the probe manufactured climbs, the plateau would not
  be flat. Every run's plateau is exactly constant by construction of
  the segmentation rule, so this cannot detect a slow drift -- it
  bounds step artifacts only.
